Projects per category

The biggest categories are Skills, plugins and rules on Best of Vibe Coding (31 projects) and Model serving on Best of Open-Source AI (20 projects).

Licenses

87% of all 322 projects use a permissive license such as MIT or Apache-2.0. 27 have custom terms or no license, so read those before you use one at work.

GPU needs

On Best of Open-Source AI, 102 of 155 projects need no GPU and 33 run faster with one; 8 require a GPU.

Docker, arm64 and live demos

Docker files or an image ship with 31% of Best of Vibe Coding and 93% of Best of Open-Source AI. You can try 69 projects in a live demo before you install anything.

Languages

The most common main language is TypeScript on Best of Vibe Coding (84) and Python on Best of Open-Source AI (73).

Last commit

240 of 322 projects (75%) had a commit in the last 30 days. None has gone a year without one.

Stars

The median project has 5k stars on Best of Vibe Coding and 20.1k stars on Best of Open-Source AI. OpenClaw has the most: 391.8k.

Scores

The median score is 50 on Best of Vibe Coding and 65 on Best of Open-Source AI, and 29 projects score 80 or more out of 100.

Method changes

3 dated changes to how we rank, newest first. The methodology page keeps the same log.

  • 2026-10-13: Maintenance weighs 15% (was 10%) and Momentum 10% (was 15%), so steady upkeep counts more than a burst of stars
  • 2026-10-12: penalties we do not check yet are marked coming; only "no license" has ever been applied
  • 2026-10-09: Docker alone earns no points, medals mark places 1–3 instead of score bands
