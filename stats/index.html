Projects per category

The biggest categories are Skills, plugins and rules on Best of Vibe Coding (53 projects) and Model serving on Best of Open-Source AI (23 projects).

Licenses

84% of all 446 projects use a permissive license such as MIT or Apache-2.0. 46 have custom terms or no license, so read those before you use one at work.

GPU needs

On Best of Open-Source AI, 110 of 231 projects need no GPU and 81 run faster with one; 14 require a GPU.

Docker, arm64 and live demos

Docker files or an image ship with 28% of Best of Vibe Coding and 71% of Best of Open-Source AI. You can try 101 projects in a live demo before you install anything.

Languages

The most common main language is TypeScript on Best of Vibe Coding (103) and Python on Best of Open-Source AI (117).

Last commit

336 of 446 projects (75%) had a commit in the last 30 days. None has gone a year without one.

Stars

The median project has 20.9k stars on Best of Vibe Coding and 31.7k stars on Best of Open-Source AI. OpenClaw has the most: 392k.

Scores

The median score is 56 on Best of Vibe Coding and 64 on Best of Open-Source AI, and 46 projects score 80 or more out of 100.

Method changes

3 dated changes to how we rank, newest first. The methodology page keeps the same log.

  • 2026-10-13: Maintenance weighs 15% (was 10%) and Momentum 10% (was 15%), so steady upkeep counts more than a burst of stars
  • 2026-10-12: penalties we do not check yet are marked coming; only "no license" has ever been applied
  • 2026-10-09: Docker alone earns no points, medals mark places 1–3 instead of score bands
