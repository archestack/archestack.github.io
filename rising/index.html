How it is counted

  • Stars gained: the sum of GitHub's daily star counts for the project over the last 7 or 30 complete days, in UTC, updated every day. GitHub counts the people who still star it, so a star taken back later drops out.
  • Growth: the stars gained divided by the stars the project had when the window started. "New" means it started at zero.
  • Only projects on our lists, each linked to its review. The place shown is its place in its category, which comes from the score, not from stars gained.
  • A window whose biggest day is followed by three days with under a tenth of its stars is left out, and a lone jump in the last three days waits until three days have followed it.
  • Not part of the score. See how the ranking works.
