Sponsor Archestack

Sponsors keep Archestack free to read. A sponsorship buys one labelled spot beside the rankings and has no effect on any rank.

What a sponsor gets

  • One spot on the site, marked "Sponsor", beside the rankings. One sponsor at a time, booked by the month.
  • One line marked "Sponsor" in the list's README on GitHub, under the note on independence.
  • One labelled line in the weekly email, once the email launches.

The rules

  • Your project keeps the rank its data gives it, whether you sponsor or not.
  • We write no sponsored reviews and change no review for a sponsor.
  • Sponsored spots stay out of tables, lists and project pages, and always carry the "Sponsor" label.
  • We decline products that mislead readers, such as a closed tool that presents itself as open source.

The numbers

  • Best of Vibe Coding: 167 projects in 19 categories, 1 star on GitHub, 30 views of the list on GitHub in the 15 days to 11 Oct 2026.
  • Best of Open-Source AI: 155 projects in 16 categories, 3 stars on GitHub, 58 views of the list on GitHub in the 15 days to 11 Oct 2026.
  • We count site visits with Umami, which sets no cookies, and send the latest month with every offer.

We update these numbers at every refresh of the lists.

Price

  • $49 a month for one list: its README on GitHub and its pages on this site.
  • $99 a month for the site and both lists.
  • The first two sponsors pay half for their first month.
  • Every three months we reset the price at about $5 per 1,000 monthly views and show you the numbers.
